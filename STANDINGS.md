# Standings, Playoffs, and Predictions

## Season standings

Choose a season in the NBA Standings section to view its regular-season records
and postseason bracket. The archive includes the playoffs played from calendar
years 2000–2026: choose 1999–00 for the 2000 playoffs and 2025–26 for the 2026
playoffs. Historical regular-season seeding rules are applied without changing
records or statistics: division champions were guaranteed top-three seeds
through 2004–05 and top-four seeds from 2005–06 through 2014–15. Historical
franchise names are shown; clean current NBA franchise logos replace unavailable
period-specific marks rather than using low-resolution wordmarks. Standings are ranked by
winning percentage and wins; available conference records and point differential
help resolve ties because ESPN's source table is grouped, not conference-ranked.
Failed ESPN archive date ranges are retried and subdivided so a transient timeout
does not discard an otherwise complete season. A date still rejected with HTTP
400 after subdivision is logged and treated as an empty date; the regular-season
completeness check still prevents an incomplete schedule from being presented
as complete.

The playoff bracket uses the selected season's regular-season conference seeds
and completed playoff game results from the schedule API. It always displays all
15 series slots and advances actual series winners through the first round,
conference semifinals, conference finals, and NBA Finals. First-round series
through the 2001–02 season are best-of-five; later rounds and later first rounds
are best-of-seven. Play-In Tournament games are excluded from series win totals.
When postseason games have not started or are unavailable, the full seeded
bracket remains visible with unresolved matchups marked accordingly.
Open **Game box scores** on a series card to browse its games and open the
individual player box score for each completed game. Historical box scores use
the selected playoff season. Archived ESPN box scores include player-specific
ESPN headshots; a player's initials portrait is shown if the image provider has
no portrait for that player.

## 2026–27 standings prediction

Drag the teams into a 1–15 order in each conference, or use the row's up/down
buttons, then choose **Save my prediction**. The prediction is stored in the
current browser's local storage; it is not tied to an account, shared with other
users, or submitted to the API. **Clear prediction** removes the saved local
prediction.
